Our Belief

The forest holds

We simply listen.

"Aranya" means forest in Sanskrit — a sacred space where nature's intelligence has accumulated over millennia. Every Aranya product is a conversation between ancient Ayurvedic texts and modern wellness science.

We never take shortcuts. Our herbs are ethically sourced, our formulations time-tested, our promise simple: give your body what the earth intended.

1994
Est. 1994 · Our Story

Three decades of
listening to nature.

What began in 1994 as a small Ayurvedic compounding kitchen in Kerala has grown into one of South India's most trusted herbal wellness brands. Aranya was founded on one conviction: that the formulations in our ancient Shastra texts are not relics — they are living medicine.

For 30 years, we have sourced the same herbs from the same forest communities, refined the same classical recipes, and delivered them with the care of a family that uses them. No shortcuts. No compromise on the original intention of Ayurveda.

Today, Aranya products reach homes across India and beyond — but every bottle still carries the soul of that original kitchen in Kerala.
